## 2. D-TDR-1 — the per-signal ratio decay-resolution law (owner rulings recorded)

**Decision.** For a strategy whose registered config declares the D-TDR-2 ratio form, the decay surface is resolved **per signal** at scoring time:

1. **The law.** `halfLifeMinutes = barsPerHalfLife × timeframeMinutes`, decay family unchanged (GreeksDecayTemplate v1 semantics: `decayModel "exp"`, `decayed = baseScore * 0.5 ^ (elapsedMinutes / halfLifeMinutes)`, unit minutes). `barsPerHalfLife` is the strategy's declared parameter; the **governed default/reference value is 12** (recorded in the schema description and here; froggy registers the default). The derived surface's identity is **`greeksTemplateId: "decay-ratio-v1"`** — a law identifier pinned by this clause, deliberately not a member of `DEFAULT_DECAY_TEMPLATES_BY_HORIZON` and not a registry entry; it marks every stamp and outcome row produced under this law (rows remain `horizonBasis: "decay-derived"` — no new basis).
2. **The parsing law.** `timeframeMinutes` derives from the canonical signal's `facts.timeframe` string by: (a) applying the governed normalization (`normalizeTimeframe` semantics, Evidence §1.2) so raw TradingView tokens and AFI forms resolve identically; (b) matching the grammar `^([1-9][0-9]*)([mM]|[hH]|[dD]|[wW])$` with multipliers m=1, h=60, d=1440, w=10080, M=43200 — `m`/`M` case-sensitive (minute vs month); `h`/`d`/`w` case-folding, so TradingView's uppercase multi-unit tokens (`2D`, `3D`, `2W`), which pass through the normalizer unchanged, resolve to their real minutes rather than falling to the assumption arm. A match whose product is a **safe integer** yields `timeframeMinutes` and a stamp flagged `timeframeAssumed: false`; an overflowing digit string is unparseable (no unsafe or infinite clock can ever stamp as parsed).
3. **The strategy-declared assumption (owner ruling, S-refined).** A non-matching, empty, `"unknown"`, or absent `facts.timeframe` resolves through the registration's declared `unknownTimeframeMinutes` when present: `timeframeMinutes = unknownTimeframeMinutes`, same law, stamp flagged `timeframeAssumed: true` — so unknown-arm rows stay segmentable (owner rider). Froggy declares `5`.
4. **The declared-nothing backstop.** A ratio-form registration that declares no `unknownTimeframeMinutes` resolves unparseable timeframes to the profile's pinned `unknownOrMissing` selection **as a value**: `{greeksTemplateId: "decay-swing-v1", halfLifeMinutes: 720}`, stamp flagged `timeframeAssumed: true`. The value is carried as a code constant **test-pinned equal to the registry file's `decaySurface.horizonSelection.unknownOrMissing` selection and its template's half-life** — the profile file is consumed at test time only; no runtime profile read begins or changes (RC-GOV boundaries untouched). Unreachable for froggy (which declares 5); it exists so the law is total.
5. **Rejected mechanisms (recorded with their merits, the AR-GOV Option-V pattern).** The lettered class-map laws put to the owner 2026-08-03 — **A** `{3,30,240}` conventional bands, **B** `{1,15,120}` bar-ratio-optimal bands, **C** `{5,30,240}` live-stream-differentiating bands, each activating `horizonSelection` as timeframe→class→template — are **rejected**: they quantize every chart to four half-lives where the ratio law yields each timeframe its own clock, and A/B could not differentiate the two live streams at all. They remain available to a future filing under its own authorization; the map stays dormant. A protocol-fixed ratio constant (Option P analogue) was likewise rejected — the parameter is analyst-declared and hash-bound (owner ruling, D-philosophy). An `unknownClass` fallback (Option U, the profile arm as universal unknown law) was rejected in favor of the strategy-declared assumption; the profile arm survives only as the D-TDR-1(4) backstop.
6. **Rider 1 — capture-fetch bound disposition (owner-commissioned).** Under the unchanged code-level 300-candle single-fetch bound (Evidence §1.7 — recorded on the ledger here for the first time), ratio-law strategies on charts slower than 2h produce capture-time **loud failures** at their outer horizons (DH-GOV's window-head assertion law guarantees loudness) until pagination lands under its own future authorization; pagination is hereby recorded as reserved. This is the recorded, accepted operating bound — not a defect, not silently capped, not a capture change.
7. **Rider 2 — self-describing stamps (owner-commissioned).** Every stamp produced under the ratio law's parsed or assumed arm carries the law's full witness: the derived `halfLifeMinutes`, the `decay-ratio-v1` identity, `barsPerHalfLife`, the `timeframeMinutes` actually used, and `timeframeAssumed` (D-TDR-3(3)); the D-TDR-1(4) backstop stamp witnesses its own law — the pinned template identity plus `timeframeAssumed: true` — so every outcome row (which echoes `decayRef {greeksTemplateId, halfLifeMinutes}` and joins the scoring-context doc by `signalId`) is self-describing about which law produced its clock.

**Scope-guard.** Fixes the resolution law, its parsing law, its identity marker, its assumption/backstop arms, the recorded rulings and riders only. It changes no template value, wires no class map, adds no outcome basis, and authorizes no implementation by itself (D-TDR-5 does).
