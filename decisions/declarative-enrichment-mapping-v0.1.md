## 2. D-DEM-2 — The mapping becomes declared data

**Decision.**

1. **A new governed contract family, `afi.enrichment-mapping.v1`,** is created in `afi-config`: `schemas/enrichment-mapping/v1/enrichment-mapping.schema.json` with `$id`, `x-afiStatus: governed-contract`, `x-afiPartOf`, `x-afiDoctrineRefs`, `x-afiConstraints` for what draft-07 cannot express, and `additionalProperties: false`; plus its `README.md`, an `examples/` document, `vectors/valid/` and `vectors/invalid/`, and an AJV `strict: true` validation test under `tests/`. FCP-GOV D-FCP-3's enumerated family (`factory-configurable-pipelines-v1.md:73-88`) is thereby **amended** to add this one schema contract and the one registry of (2), routed through that clause's own change-control sentence (`:89`) and leaving its scope-guard (`:91`) otherwise intact. The name `afi.enrichment-mapping.v1` sits **outside** the D-FCP-1 five-category enrichment namespace (`afi.enrichment.<category>.v1`) and confers no category identity; it names a mapping document, never a lane.
2. **Mapping documents are registry records** at `afi-config/registries/enrichment-mappings/<mappingId>--<version>.json`, following the existing registry conventions in full: one file per identity, immutability, supersede-by-new-version, pinned-contents drift guard, and an entry in `tests/registries-seeding-validation.test.ts`.
3. **Selection is per strategy, by reference.** The analyst-strategy-config gains `mappingRef: { mappingId, version }` — the fourth `*Ref` alongside `pipelineRef`, `scorerRef`, `uwrProfileRef`. Because `analyst-strategy-config/v1` is `additionalProperties: false` (`:24`), this is a **governed-schema amendment in two places**: the afi-config source of truth and the reactor's vendored closure copy (`afi-reactor/src/pipeline/governed-schema/analyst-strategy-config.schema.json`), with the vendored-closure drift guard (`afi-reactor/test/pipeline/vendoredSchemaProvenance.test.ts`) green.
4. **Layer roles follow FCP-GOV D-FCP-4 and MATH-GOV.** The contract lives in `afi-config`; the deterministic **mapping interpreter** — a pure, total, side-effect-free kernel over a validated mapping document and an enriched view — lives in `afi-core` as an SDK/validator surface (MATH-GOV `math-authority-v0.1.md:55,:58`), redefining no `afi-math` kernel (`:104-105`); `afi-reactor` resolves the registered mapping and invokes the interpreter at the scorer seam. Mapping **content** remains strategy-local (`:100`, `:109`) and is registered, never promoted. No mapping semantics may live in the reactor, and no I/O may live in the interpreter.
5. **Merge order is binding and is a consequence of how CI resolves cross-repo dependencies.** `afi-reactor` CI checks out three siblings at their **default branches with no `ref:`** — afi-core (`afi-reactor/.github/workflows/validate-all.yml:21-25`), afi-math (`:27-31`), afi-config (`:33-37`) — and afi-core/afi-config are `file:` path dependencies installed as symlinks. afi-math is untouched by this program and imposes no ordering constraint. Therefore: **(a)** `afi-core` merges first (the interpreter, referenced by nothing); **(b)** `afi-config` merges second with the `mappingRef` schema amendment **optional**, no registration; **(c)** `afi-reactor` merges third, re-vendoring the closure **pinned to the step-(b) commit** and accepting `mappingRef` while tolerating its absence; **(d)** `afi-config` merges fourth with the mapping registry family and the froggy registration; **(e)** a final bounded step declares `mappingRef` **required** once every consumer accepts it. **No reactor re-vendor may pin an afi-config commit that is not on afi-config's `main`** — the provenance guard is a hardcoded string compare and a hash of the vendored bytes (`vendoredSchemaProvenance.test.ts:23-33`); it consults no live afi-config checkout and would pass green on a false pin, which is precisely the falsehood it exists to prevent. A slot PR that lands a required field before its consumers accept it turns `main` red in another repository and must not merge.
6. **Fail-closed resolution.** An unresolvable, unregistered, or schema-invalid `mappingRef` refuses at resolution and yields no determination and no record, per FCP-GOV D-FCP-5's "fails closed for unknown, unregistered, or inactive identities" (`factory-configurable-pipelines-v1.md:114`) and "no silent identity fallback" (`:116`). There is no built-in mapping, no default mapping, and no code-path fallback to the retired bespoke code.
7. **Retirement is bounded by expressibility.** The bespoke adapter is retired **only to the extent its behavior is expressible** under D-DEM-3. What remains after `DEM-BIND` is named, bounded, and enumerated: the `liquiditySwept` computation (D-DEM-3(5)), the five placeholders until their producer slots land (D-DEM-4(2)), and the descriptor defaults of Evidence §12. Nothing else may remain, and nothing may be added.

**Scope-guard.** Fixes the contract family, its home, its carrier, the layer roles, and the merge order. It fixes no mapping *content*, registers no mapping, changes no scorer, and by itself moves no scored value. It creates no second executor, no second registry mechanism, and no new evidence-record member.

## 3. D-DEM-3 — The bound: mapping is binding, not computation

**Decision.**

1. **A mapping document declares bindings, and nothing else.** The complete operator vocabulary is exactly five forms, each total and each decidable from the document alone: **(a) `bind`** — read one declared source path within the enriched view into one declared target input, with a declared type; **(b) `default`** — a declared literal used when the bound source is absent, permitted only on a binding declared `optional` under D-DEM-5(3)/(4); **(c) `band`** — map a numeric source onto an ordinal target by an ordered, exhaustive, non-overlapping threshold table (the form EQ-GOV D-EQ-2 already fixes), **with an explicitly declared absent-source member**, the way (d) already provides; **(d) `recode`** — map a source string onto a closed target union by an explicit total table with a declared fallback member (the form AR-GOV D-AR-3 already fixes); **(e) `namespace-default`** — an absent lane namespace resolves as the declared empty object so that bindings beneath it resolve as *absent* rather than throwing.
2. **A mapping may never express computation.** Expressly prohibited in any mapping document: arithmetic of any kind; composition of two or more source facts into one target; series, window, or time-based derivation; conditionals other than the two total tables of (c) and (d); I/O, network, clock, or randomness; and — restating FCP-GOV D-FCP-2(5) (`factory-configurable-pipelines-v1.md:54`) rather than amending it — "**no `eval`, no arbitrary code, no user-supplied executable expressions**". A mapping may bind **only** to a fact already projected into the enriched view; a path that does not resolve to a declared projected field is invalid at registration, not at runtime.
3. **The escape hatch is a registered producer, and it is the only one.** Anything computational is a **plugin** under FCP-GOV D-FCP-3's analysis-plugin contract, running inside its lane, whose result is projected into the enriched view as a declared fact that the mapping then binds. The naming rule is symmetric and binding: **no computation may live in a mapping, and no mapping-shaped constant may live in a producer.** A producer that emits a literal in place of a computation it has not implemented is the same defect D-DEM-4 prohibits, wherever it sits. This filing creates **no** join-scoped or merge-seam producer category: AR-GOV D-AR-1 rejected merge-seam derivation as doctrine-violating (`atr-regime-v0.1.md:41`, non-authorization `:107`), and that rejection stands unamended.
4. **This filing states its own insufficiency plainly.** Against the ten inputs of Evidence §2, the vocabulary of (1) expresses **four** today (`distanceFromDailyEmaPct`, `pulledBackIntoSweetSpot`, `triggerPatternQuality`, `atrRegime`) and can reach a further handful of already-projected-but-unread facts (`pattern.structureBias`, `pattern.trendPullbackConfirmed`, `technical.atr14`, `technical.trendBias`). It expresses **none** of the five placeholders, because four of them have no producer and one has no carried provider fact (Evidence §5). **A declarative contract therefore removes no placeholder by itself.** D-DEM-4's prohibition is discharged only by the producer slots of §9, and a program that lands the contract and calls the placeholder problem solved has not discharged it.
5. **`liquiditySwept` stays exactly where it is, and this filing does not relocate it.** Under (2) it is not expressible as a mapping. Under (3) it has no lawful producer: it reads `pattern.patternName` **and** `sentiment.tags` (Evidence §4), two lanes, and the only join seam is the merge node, whose derivation D-AR-1 rejected. It therefore remains a named, bounded, strategy-local computation at the analyst seam, **outside the mapping**, inert by FLPR-GOV D-FLPR-5(2) and guarded at `fiveLaneAdapters.test.ts:892`. No slot of §9 may bind it in a mapping, approximate it, relocate it to the merge seam, or change its value by one byte. Its disposition — a single-lane producer, a join-scoped producer category reconciled against D-AR-1, or removal — is expressly reserved to its own filing.
6. **A registration may bind a currently-unread projected field only under its own authorization.** The initial froggy mapping reads exactly what the adapter reads today, so AR-GOV D-AR-3's scope-guard ("the four projected context fields … remain unread — the inertness guard … must pass **unchanged**") is honored without amendment. A future registration that binds one of them amends that guard under its own filing.

**Scope-guard.** Fixes the operator vocabulary, the prohibition, the producer boundary, and the named residue. It fixes no band edge, no recode table entry, no default literal, and no source path — those are mapping *content*, registered per strategy. It grants no mapping the power to reach a fact the projection does not carry, and it authorizes no projection change; a producer slot may extend a lane projection only for the facts its own bindings require, and only under that slot's authorization.

## 5. D-DEM-5 — Full-fidelity axes, and the law when an input is genuinely unavailable

**Decision.**

1. **Every declared UWR axis is computed from real enrichment.** No axis input on the production scoring path may take its value from a compile-time constant, a literal in an adapter or a lane projection, or a proxy synthesized from another axis input. This executes the owner ruling of 2026-08-12 that all four axes must always be live and informative, and it names the current **risk-axis two-value collapse (`{0.2, 0.9}`) and structure-axis `0.40` cap** (Evidence §3) as **defects**, not as design.
2. **A required binding whose source is absent fails closed: no score, no record.** This is the chosen law, and the alternative is expressly rejected. D-FCP-8's second bullet — "optional nodes degrade only per a declared fail-soft policy … and every such degradation is **recorded** — never silent" (`:163`) — governs *optional nodes*, and a required scorer input is by construction not an optional node. Recorded fail-soft on a required axis input would still emit a number that the record presents in the same field, in the same units, as numbers computed from complete inputs; the immutable-record law exists precisely so that a stored score means one thing. CFG-GOV D-CFG-1's "No fabricated completeness" (`analyst-configuration-freedom-v0.1.md:45`) already settles it: a determination that could not be computed as composed yields **no record**.
3. **The only lawful successor to a constant is a declared optional binding, and a fired default is committed to a hash.** A binding may be declared `optional` **in the mapping document only**, and only with a declared default literal that is part of the hashed mapping identity (D-DEM-6). When such a default fires, that fact enters the **`executionSummaryHash` preimage** for that determination, as a degradation marker — the element `afi.composition-ref.v1` already admits (`afi-config/schemas/composition-ref/v1/composition-ref.schema.json:19`). The effect is precise and limited: **two determinations that differ only in whether a default fired are hash-distinguishable forever, and are not reader-legible.** The execution summary is not a record member (Evidence §8), so a reader sees a hash, not the fact. Making a fired default reader-visible would require a new evidence-record member, which D-DEM-6(2) and §8 forbid; that disclosure is **expressly reserved to its own Tier F filing**. This moves `executionSummaryHash` **values** where a default fires; it changes no hash law and amends no `x-afiConstraints` text.
4. **Optionality has two grounds and one grandfather, and nothing else.** A binding may be declared optional **only** where: **(a)** the fact is legitimately absent for a **composition** reason — a lane the analyst did not select, per CFG-GOV D-CFG-3's composition scoping; or **(b)** the fact is one a **selected lane's registered producer may legitimately not emit for a given input** — insufficient history, an undefined indicator at the series edge, a pattern the detector did not find — declared as such in the producer's own contract and enumerated in the mapping. **Grandfather (exhaustive, non-extensible):** the four absent-source defaults the retired seam already applies — `technical.emaDistancePct ?? 0` (`froggy.enrichment_adapter.ts:225`), `technical.isInValueSweetSpot ?? false` (`:226`), the `patternConfidence` absent branch (`:232-235`), and the `atrRegime` default-to-`"normal"` fallback (`:250-255`, the form AR-GOV D-AR-3 fixed) — are admitted as declared optional bindings with their existing literals so that `DEM-BIND` reproduces today's scored values exactly. **No new optional binding may be created on the grandfather's ground**, and **a fact whose producer merely does not exist yet is never optional; it is a defect.** No slot may satisfy its gate by declaring a placeholder optional.
5. **Informativeness is an obligation, and its remedy is reserved.** No declared axis may be **constant, capped below its declared output range, or a deterministic function of another axis's bindings**, over the reachable domain of its bindings. Where evidence shows such a condition — as Evidence §3 shows for `risk` against `structure`, and for the structure cap at `0.40` — the filing that lands the producer must **record** the residual defect; its remedy is a formula and therefore belongs to a modelling filing, which this is not.
6. **A submitter-asserted quantity may not buy score.** No mapping may bind, and no producer may emit as its own output, a value whose sole origin is an assertion by the party who submitted the signal, unless that value is **verifiable against observed market data** and is validated as such by the producer that emits it (for example, a submitted stop or target expressed as a price, checked against the candles the technical lane actually fetched, and refused when it cannot be checked). This is DIR-GOV D-DIR-3's reasoning applied to quantities rather than to sides: D-DIR-3 barred the submitted direction from any bias input because it "would grant every signal a free lift merely for asserting a side" and "would collapse the analyst/provider role split" (`scored-signal-direction-restoration-v0.1.md:65`), and the standing owner ruling is that the provider only feeds opportunities while the analyst configures enrichment and rubric. `rrMultiplePlanned` is the sole driver of the risk axis (`froggy.trend_pullback_v1.ts:99-120`), so an unverified submitted plan would purchase that axis outright. `DEM-PRODUCER-PLAN` is bound by this clause and may not merge without satisfying it.
7. **Fail-closed applies at resolution as well as at determination.** An unresolvable or invalid mapping refuses before any determination is attempted (D-DEM-2(6)); an absent required source refuses at the determination that needs it. Neither ever produces a partial record, and neither ever falls back.

**Scope-guard.** Fixes the fidelity obligation, the unavailable-input law, the bounded optionality mechanism, where a degradation is committed, and the bar on unverified submitter assertions. It sets no axis formula, no weight, no clamp, no default literal, and no threshold; it does not decide which inputs are required for any given strategy — that is mapping content; and it authorizes no change to the evidence record's members or to any `x-afiConstraints` text (the execution summary's *contents* move, its contract does not).

## 6. D-DEM-6 — Mapping identity, hashing, immutability, and what fixtures pin

**Decision.**

1. **A mapping version is immutable once any signal has been scored under it**, exactly as CFG-GOV D-CFG-5(1) provides for a configuration version (`analyst-configuration-freedom-v0.1.md:94`). Changes register a **new** mapping version; a version under which determinations exist is never edited. A mapping document is hashed by the existing canonical-json hashing discipline (`canonical-json-hashing.v1`), and its hash is pinned in the registry the way the other registry families pin theirs.
2. **Mapping identity reaches the record through the configuration, with no new record member.** Because `mappingRef` is a member of the analyst-strategy-config document, and that document is the `analystConfigHash` preimage, the mapping identity enters every record's `compositionRef` automatically. **Nothing is added to the `uwrProfile` stamp** — it is `additionalProperties: false` with a fixed required set in three repositories (Evidence §8), and widening it is a Tier F act this filing does not take.
3. **What moves is hash values, never hash law.** Adding `mappingRef` to the config moves `analystConfigHash` for the existing registration, and therefore `recordHash` and `replayHash`; a fired default moves `executionSummaryHash` for that determination (D-DEM-5(3)). `afi.hash.v1`, both disciplines, every exclusion list, and every hashing KAT are **byte-identical**. EV3-GOV D-EV3-4's hash law and D-EV3-7's admission contract are untouched: records carrying the new values admit unchanged.
4. **Resolution is exact and permanent.** A record resolves to exactly one mapping version, because the configuration version it names is immutable under D-CFG-5(1) and the mapping version that configuration names is immutable under (1). Two records scored under different mappings are distinguishable by `analystConfigHash` alone; no reader needs the mapping document to know that they differ.
5. **Fixtures pin the pair, per CFG-GOV D-CFG-6 (`analyst-configuration-freedom-v0.1.md:101-103`).** Where a golden, KAT, or oracle fixture pins a scored value, it pins that value against a named configuration identity — which now includes the mapping identity. Re-recording names the mapping the fixture already assumes and **moves no scored-value byte**; any regeneration documents field-level intentional diffs in `afi-reactor/test/oracle/INTENTIONAL_DIFFS.md` in the established house form (per-golden old → new table, an explicit byte-EQUAL statement for everything else, the recursive-JSON-path-differ audit method, and the closing rule "Anything else diffing would have been a defect to fix in code, never absorbed into a golden"). The `312da118…126e06` provenance golden and its sha256 pin (`test/guardrails/districtSurfaceConsolidation.test.ts:28-30,152-158`) remain **byte-identical**; no re-pin is authorized by this filing.
6. **Tier.** Registering a *new mapping version* that moves no persisted scored value, no golden/KAT/oracle byte, no governed schema, and no economic surface is **Tier S** under CFG-GOV D-CFG-5(2) (`:95`) — it is the exercise of a governed configuration surface. Changing the mapping *schema*, the interpreter, the operator vocabulary, or any existing registration's scored values remains **Tier F** under D-CFG-5(3) (`:96`), and GPR-GOV D-GPR-3(2) applies in full: a change believed Tier S that turns out to move a score, hash, or golden byte **stops and upgrades to Tier F**.

**Scope-guard.** Fixes how a mapping is identified, hashed, made immutable, and pinned by fixtures. It adds no evidence-record member, changes no hash law, creates no new fixture family, re-pins no existing golden sha256, and does not relax the byte-stability gate for any existing composition.

**Scope-guard.** Movement is bounded to the axes fed by the named inputs of an owner-authorized slot, arising from that slot's registered producer. Any other scored-value movement — from a changed axis formula, a changed weight, a changed clamp, a changed band edge, a changed recode entry, a re-pinned provenance golden, or any formula other than the slot's own producer — is outside this authorization, and a program producing it must not merge.
